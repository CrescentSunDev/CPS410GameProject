The company has given you an implant, allowing your manager to communicate with you no matter the distance(this could be done as pre-mission briefing text a la Cruelty Squad, or a overlay at the beginning of the level such as a little 'video-call' screen in the corner). The campaign will start on mercury, and levels will work sequentially outward from the 'core' of the solar system.

In this act, you are given your last assignment, which will clear your debt to the company. All you have to do is bring a nondescript cylinder to a client on Luna. Levels involved will be Mercury, Venus, Earth, then Luna.
