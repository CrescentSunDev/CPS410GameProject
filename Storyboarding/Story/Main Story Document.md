The company has given you an implant, allowing your manager to communicate with you no matter the distance(this could be done as pre-mission briefing text a la [Cruelty Squad](https://gameverse.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/cruelty-squad-screenshot-7-1920px.jpg) (obviously not as insane looking, but the ui element of a text box and talking gif of your handler), or a overlay at the beginning of the level such as a little ['video-call'](https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/chorus/uploads/chorus_asset/file/15401652/Screen_Shot_2015-06-15_at_9.24.17_PM.0.0.1434421689.png?quality=90&strip=all&crop=8.930669800235%2C0%2C82.13866039953%2C100&w=2400) screen in the corner). The campaign will start on mercury, and levels will work sequentially outward from the 'core' of the solar system. 

## Act 1 - Mercury to Luna
In this act, you are given your last assignment, which will clear your debt to the company. All you have to do is bring a nondescript cylinder to a client on Pluto, with other packages to drop off along the way. Levels involved will be Mercury, Venus, Earth, then Luna. 

### Mercury
This level will serve as the tutorial, and for it your objective is to bring your manager's connection gear from the company building to your ship. You will have to do basic platforming and try out all the main mechanics to get acquainted with them. The level itself will look mostly like well-used or rusty industrial space once you leave the corporate building. Once you reach the hangar, you place the manager's gear with the engineers and board your ship to await takeoff.

### Venus
As you reach Venus, you land on a city floating in the clouds. Your manager informs you that you must deliver a package to a client nearby, but other corporations have closed off the route through the city itself. You must jump across infrastructure such as solar panels, balloons, helicopter drones and girders to scale the complex in order to reach the client. Once on the client's level of the building, your manager says you must dodge overzealous security who want to look in your package. After getting around the security measures, and into the clients office, they thank you for the package and offer to refill your ship for free as a tip. As you return to the hangar and your ship lifts off in the end level cutscene, an explosion can be seen in the clients office.

### Luna
On Luna, you travel through a warzone to get to the pristine looking capital. Once in the capital, you are approached by a member of the Lunarii People's Front, a revolutionary group involved in the fighting outside the Lunar capital. They explain to you that you are not just dropping off packages, but are then cut off by your manager calling corporate forces over, forcing them to flee. You continue on to deliver to the client at your manager's orders.


## Act 2 - Luna to Jupiter
This act will be centered around the travels from luna out to jupiter. 
### Mars
Upon arriving on mars, you are greeted by massive underground farms and cities. The level layout would be something like tunnels leading through the ground (possibly dodge drill machines or minecarts/trains?) and letting out in an underground farm area. For ease of making platforming sections, we could make the farm a multilevel structure. The man you have to deliver to will be at the other end of the farm/cavern in a management building which seems to be the edge between the farm and the urban space/cavern. Possible hazards could be: farming combines, farmer drones(could swing a scythe around or something), yo\kels with shotguns and overalls, tunnel drills, leftover 'farmified' assets from previous levels. We could also reference futurama  by having bug cattle to use as moving platforms.

Your Goal is to deliver an invoice for 'fertilizer' which is definitely not explosive material. 

### Ganymede
Your next stop takes you to Ganymede, Jupiter's largest moon and the center of the Jovian Dukedom, a squabbling set of petty lords and rich nobles that have made their own nation throughout Jupiter's moons. The company wants you to deliver pipes, metal bits and bobs, and crates full of gunpowder marked for construction usage. The recipient of this delivery is not a member of the Jovian Dukedom, but a peasant leader/foreman. The enemies/hazards in this area could be things like knights on robot horses, futuristic pikemen, and construction worksite hazards. The map itself should look future-medieval, a-la neon signs on tudor houses and castles (or similar but less distracing sci-fi greebles). The route the player takes should start on a landing pad, and route through a construction site that is currently being raided by the Jovian Dukedom. Once the packages are delivered/airdropped, the peasants will quickly assemble them into weapons and race offscreen, shooting in the direction of Dukedom forces (or just to the right/away from the player)
